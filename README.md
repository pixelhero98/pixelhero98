# Zinuo (Henry) You

Generative systems researcher working on **video and world models**, **efficient generative inference**, and **continuous-time learning**.

I am a Computer Science PhD candidate at the University of Bristol, leading modelling research and implementation across UKRI-funded projects. My work connects mathematical formulation, large-scale evaluation, and release-quality research software.

## Selected work

- **[GenODE / GICO](https://github.com/pixelhero98/GenODE-Inference)** — context-conditioned integration clocks for frozen generative ODE and flow-matching backbones.
- **[Latent Laplace Diffusion](https://github.com/pixelhero98/LLapDiffusion)** — parallel generative forecasting over irregular timestamps; ICML 2026 Spotlight.
- **[Diffusion-to-Flow Schedule Transfer](https://github.com/pixelhero98/Diffusion-Flow-Inference)** — controlled transfer and evaluation of diffusion schedules in normalized flow time.
- **[AuditOps](https://github.com/pixelhero98/AuditOps)** — public foundations for canonical evidence, deterministic validation, and restricted-domain audit benchmarks.
